Modules

Mr. Edward van Niekerk teaches modules focused on web development, academic communication, and professional ethics, helping students build both technical and professional skills.

Web Programming

Introduction to modern web development concepts including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, and interactive user interfaces.

ACW181

Academic Writing

Develops communication, research, referencing, and academic writing skills required for professional and university environments.

ETH271

Professional Ethics

Explores ethical decision-making, responsibility, and professional conduct within the technology and business sectors.
