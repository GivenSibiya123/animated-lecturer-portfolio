About Mr. van Niekerk

A seasoned academic and Department Head at Belgium Campus iTversity, Mr. Edward van Niekerk brings structure, insight, and a deeply interactive teaching philosophy to every classroom. With years of experience in Business Science education, he shapes future professionals through rigorous engagement, ethical grounding, and a precise, perceptive approach to learning.

Teaching Philosophy

Real understanding comes through dialogue. Every lecture is designed to challenge, question, and engage students at a deeper level than passive instruction allows.

Academic Leadership

As Department Head for Business Science, Mr. van Niekerk oversees curriculum quality, module delivery, and the academic development of students across multiple programmes.

Professional Values

Structure. Integrity. Authenticity. These principles guide every interaction — from lecture design to one-on-one consultations with students navigating their academic journey.

Modules Taught

Mr. van Niekerk delivers three core modules designed to build critical academic and professional skills across the Business Science programme at Belgium Campus iTversity.

WPR161

Word Processing

A foundational module equipping students with professional document creation skills, formatting standards, and productivity tools essential for the modern workplace.

ACW181

Academic Writing

Develops structured, evidence-based writing skills. Students learn to research, argue, and communicate ideas with clarity and academic rigour across multiple formats.

ETH271

Ethics

Explores ethical frameworks, professional responsibility, and decision-making in business contexts — preparing students to navigate complex moral challenges in their careers.
